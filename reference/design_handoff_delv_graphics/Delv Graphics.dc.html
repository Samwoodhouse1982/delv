Five marks for the argument the site already makes, and the small set they are built from.

Conceptual, not evidential. No figures, no axes, nothing that could be mistaken for a result. Each one carries a single idea, drawn from the same small vocabulary: a field of marks, a boundary, and one thing that survives.

01  Distillation Home hero band — activity thins out until one thing is left worth counting. Animated: a slow drift.
02  Same ink, one form Before / After band — the scatter gathers into a single body. Nothing is added; it is the same material, made legible.

04  One finding, more than one job What we do, Articulate — the same finding leaves the source three times and fills three differently shaped containers. Nothing is restated that was not there.
05  The engagement, drawing itself How we work — four states of one claim, each more resolved than the last. The line carries on past the final mark, because the page's point is that it keeps working after they leave.
